## Problem Statement

固定使用者目前還算滿意，但 Marvin 同時長出三條分支（實體化／DJ／Discord 陪伴），需要一句話講清楚它是什麼，後續延伸才有依據。

近 30 天資料（`records/judge_outcomes.jsonl`、`records/speak_outcomes.jsonl`，已排除 test speaker）：
- 主動指令 220 次，其中約 150 次是音樂（點歌 118、個人歌單 18、播放控制 9、音量 7）；問答 9 次、笑話 4 次。
- Marvin 自己主動發話 710 次，是被叫的 3 倍。
- 核心使用者是兩個人（狗與露 30/30 天、showay 25/30 天），另外兩位大約每週來一次。
- 使用者說的 whoa 時刻是「歌選得剛好」，剛好在**跟當下話題／氣氛對上**。

## 定位（本次結論）

**Marvin 是這群朋友聊天房的電台主持人。歌負責情緒，歌與歌之間的空檔是它的舞台。**

使用者原話：「歌與歌之間的空間就是表演的舞台，說的話要跟上話題或音樂點播，例如加一些罐頭音效炒熱氣氛呢」

三條分支不是平行的產品線，而是同一件事的不同部分：
- 記憶 → 主持素材的來源（誰點的、誰最近怎樣）
- DJ → 舞台本身
- 實體化 → 換一個場地（車上、桌上），主持人不變

## What Makes This Cool

現有的 AI 主持人都是對一個人講「這首歌」。YouTube Music 的 AI 主持人講的是歌曲冷知識和故事，評論者擔心會瞎編或變成湊時間的廢話，而且被批評不能關（[Dexerto](https://www.dexerto.com/youtube/youtube-musics-new-ai-host-talks-between-songs-and-you-cant-turn-it-off-3259043/)、[Technology.org](https://www.technology.org/2025/09/29/youtubes-ai-radio-host-talks-between-your-songs-and-you-cant-turn-it-off/)）。Discord 音效機器人則要靠斜線指令或文字關鍵字觸發（[Blerp](https://blerp.com/discord-soundboard-bot)、[instants.meme](https://instants.meme/blog-post/auto-play-discord-sounds-via-keywords/)）。

Marvin 聽得到整個房間，所以它的串場素材可以是真實的對話，而且它是頻道裡唯一一直在聽的人，由它來按音效剛好。這個差異是我們根據上面的觀察推論出來的，沒有做完整的競品調查。

## Constraints

- **8/25 停用的原因**：`_play_dj_tail_sfx`（[cogs/music_cog_tail_dj.py:641](../../cogs/music_cog_tail_dj.py)）被一行 early `return` 停用，理由是懷疑「SFX 疊播（scratch 動態合成 + ffmpeg fork）跟換歌 preload 疊在同一個時間窗」造成 Discord 斷續。8/29 查到斷續的真根因是 launchd 背景 QoS（送幀 gap 94→25ms）。
- **靜態音效目前一樣會 fork ffmpeg**：`play_dj_on_tts_layer(path, peak=0.1)`（[cogs/voice_controller_playback.py:896](../../cogs/voice_controller_playback.py)）每次播都呼叫 `_ffmpeg_to_f32` 解碼。只把 scratch 拔掉，並不能讓換歌窗口裡不 fork ffmpeg。
- **現在的送幀 gap**：`bot_stdout.log` 的 `[Plan12_Stats] gap_max_ms`，最近 2000 筆（每 5 秒一筆）p50=29.8、p95=29.9、max=64.3。換歌時偶爾到 ~50ms 是已知的殘留問題（見 `incident_discord_popping_launchd_qos_2026-08-29`）。
- **笑聲偵測不能用**：6/23 驗證過不可行（STT 在 1129 句裡只認出 1 個「哈哈哈」，聲學偵測沒有標籤可驗證）。`atmosphere_tracker` 的 `has_laugh` 一樣是看 STT 文字。
- **沒有現成的即時熱度程式碼**：`crosstalk.py` 已在 `1ae7668` 刪除。可以用的即時資料是 `atmosphere_tracker._window`（最近 10 分鐘每一句的 speaker、ts），由 [cogs/voice_controller.py:1184](../../cogs/voice_controller.py) 每句餵入。
- **DJ 尾段的呼叫順序**：`_run_tail_dj` → `_start_music_preload` → `_resolve_tail_dj_meta`，拿不到 meta 就 return，**不會走到 SFX**。拿到 meta 才執行 `_maybe_play_dj_interjection`（口白，私語模式時跳過），然後 `_play_dj_tail_sfx`。所以 SFX 一定排在口白後面、同一條 TTS 層佇列。
- **專案規則**：STT 層零費用；事實類素材不讓 LLM 自由發揮；晚上 8 點到 12 點非緊急不重啟；實作照 CLAUDE.md 發包給 sonnet，收回來要自己驗；device 端程式碼改完要 scp 部署才算數。

## Premises（已同意）

1. **定位**：Marvin 是聊天房的電台主持人。記憶和實體化都在服務主持，不是平行的產品線。
2. **素材來自房間，不是歌曲百科**：串場要接得上剛剛的對話、點歌的人、大家的記憶，不做歌曲冷知識。
3. **音效要「反應」，不要「固定」**，而且要稀少。
4. **先驗證舊限制，再加新功能**。這次實際驗證的是：「**預先解碼好的靜態音效**在換歌窗口推進 mixer，不會讓送幀 gap 變差」。原本「scratch 即時合成造成斷續」的假說**不重測**，scratch 直接移除。
5. **（依第二意見修正）成功要看雙邊**：觸發後有人接話的比例要高於對照組，**而且**負面反應不能增加。音效用在**熱的時候加溫**，不用在冷場時救場。

修正第 5 條的依據：Marvin 冷場時主動開話題（ProactiveTopicAgent／idle_tick）187 次，只有 29% 之後有人接話；接在有人講話之後的類型（Bridge、MemoryCallback）則是 86–93%。`had_followup_stt` 是弱訊號，房間本來就熱時自然會偏高。

## Cross-Model Perspective（Claude subagent，冷讀）

- **最酷的版本**：這群朋友自己的聲音音效板，從本人的招牌笑聲、口頭禪切出 1–2 秒片段，梗被接住時播出來。
- **最關鍵的一句話**：「舞台」和「表演」。音效只是第一個想到的道具，驗收要看「有沒有演出來」。
- **現成的一半**：Plan12 mixer 加上 `assets/dj_sfx`。它建議的笑聲偵測器因為前述驗證牆而**不採用**。
- **挑戰前提 5**：最可能的失敗是「太吵，大家默默不理」。已採納，前提 5 已修正。

## Approaches Considered

### Approach A：歌間空檔看場合挑音效（採用）
只有在房間熱的時候，DJ 尾段才加一支音效，預設不放。Effort S，Risk Low。Completeness 6/10：音效和口白各自獨立，沒有「梗講完 → ba-dum-tss」的節奏卡點。

### Approach B：空檔變成一段節目（延後）
把 select_mode 升級成 segment planner，TTS 和 SFX 預渲染到同一條時間軸。延後原因：還沒證明有人買單。

### Approach C：朋友聲音音效板（延後）
延後原因：錄音同意、ZDR、公開處刑風險，而且片段品質無法自動驗證。

### 已否決：笑聲觸發
STT 抓不到笑聲，也沒有標籤可以驗證。

## Recommended Approach（A 的規格）

### 名詞定義
- **一次點火（fire）**：`_run_tail_dj` 每次被呼叫、執行到 `_resolve_tail_dj_meta` 之後，就算一次，不論有沒有拿到 meta。
- **真人點播**（純函式 `is_human_request(info) -> bool`）：`requested_by` 非空、不等於 `'未知'`、不是以 `'Marvin'` 開頭，**而且** `info.get('_lane') is None`。
  依據：autopilot、主題歌單、關聯選曲、故事弧、個人歌單都會設 `_lane`（`themed`／`associative`／`story_arc`／`personal`／cand.lane）。其中 autopilot 可能保留真人的名字（`_attribution_with_suki`），個人歌單則用使用者名字（`music_cog_personal_shuffle.py:120`），只看 `requested_by` 會誤判。真人點歌的路徑（`music_cog.py:1157`、`music_cog_commands.py:156`）不設 `_lane`。
- **房間熱度**（純函式 `room_heat(entries, now, window_s) -> (utt_per_min, n_speakers)`）：輸入是 `atmosphere_tracker._window` 最近 `window_s` 秒的 `(speaker, ts)`。runtime 和校準都用這個來源，所以兩者的母體相同。

### 選音效規則（純函式 `pick_tail_sfx(...) -> (name | None, reason)`）
依序判斷：
1. 私語模式（`vc._intimate_mode`）→ `None`，原因 `intimate`
2. 這次點火沒有口白（拿不到 meta）→ `None`，原因 `no_narration`（實際上根本走不到 SFX，只為了記錄完整）
3. 房間不熱（`utt_per_min < HEAT_UTT_PER_MIN` 或 `n_speakers < 2`）→ `None`，原因 `cold`
4. 冷卻中（同一支音效距上次 < `SFX_SAME_COOLDOWN_S`，或過去一小時已放 ≥ `SFX_HOURLY_CAP` 次）→ `None`，原因 `cooldown`
5. 保留對照組：以機率 `HOLDOUT_P` → `None`，原因 `holdout`
6. 真人點播 → `shoutout`（台呼：「Yo，DJ Maaaarvin！」）；否則 → `riser`

所以每一支音效都要**房間熱**才會放（符合前提 3、5）。shoutout 是台呼，不負責點名；點名已經由口白的 `_dj_requester_suffix` 處理。它只是讓真人點的歌進場時多一點儀式感。scratch 從輪替池移除；dj_airhorn 維持移除。

### 常數（暫定值，Step 2 發包前要依 Step 1 資料定案）
| 常數 | 暫定值 | 定案方式 |
|---|---|---|
| `HEAT_WINDOW_S` | 180 | 固定 |
| `HEAT_UTT_PER_MIN` | 6.0 | Step 1 記錄的熱度分布中，`n_speakers ≥ 2` 的點火取 P75 |
| `SFX_SAME_COOLDOWN_S` | 1200（20 分鐘） | 固定 |
| `SFX_HOURLY_CAP` | 3 | 固定 |
| `HOLDOUT_P` | 0.3 | 固定 |

### 播放（IO shell）
- **不在點火當下 fork ffmpeg**：`riser.wav`、`shoutout.wav` 在第一次需要時解碼成 f32 並做 `peak_normalize_f32(target_peak=0.1)`，快取在記憶體（兩個檔加起來約 4.3 秒，很小）。之後直接 `self._mixer.push_tts(f32)`。要在 `voice_controller_playback.py` 加一個方法，例如 `play_cached_sfx(name)`，和 `play_dj_on_tts_layer` 共用 `_ensure_mixer_playing` 這段。
- **響度**：沿用 PR#93 的規則。SFX 不是講話，不套 `TTS_LOUDNESS_AF`，維持 `peak=0.1`；最後由 PR#94 的 mixer 輸出限幅器把關。驗證時實聽一次，確認音量不會比口白突兀。
- **puck 分支維持關閉**：`_play_dj_tail_sfx` 原本的 puck `sfx` 呼叫這次不啟用（用明確的條件擋掉並加註解），因為要動到硬體就得另外 scp 部署、做實機驗證。

### 記錄
- 路徑：`records/dj_tail_fires.jsonl`（`records/` 已在 `.gitignore:63`）。
- 每次點火寫一筆：`ts`、`next_title`、`is_human_request`、`has_narration`、`utt_per_min`、`n_speakers`、`present_speakers`、`sfx`（名稱或 `null`）、`reason`。**不存逐字稿。**
- 保存層級：和 `records/speak_outcomes.jsonl`（同樣記 `present_speakers`）相同。
- 「之後有沒有人接話」和「之後有沒有負面反應」**不在 runtime 算**，改用離線腳本做 join，就不會多出新的 async task：
  - 接話：`pipeline_timing.jsonl` 在 `(ts, ts+10s]` 內有任何非 test speaker 的一筆。
  - 負面反應：`judge_outcomes.jsonl` 在 `(ts, ts+60s]` 內，`winner_name == 'frustration'`，或 `raw_query` 符合 `閉嘴|安靜|好吵|吵死|小聲|不要放音效`。**`playback_control`（停止播放、切歌）不算負面**，因為那是正常的音樂操作。

## Success Criteria

上線滿一週（約 2026-10-09）看：

1. **斷音沒有復發（硬門檻）**：在每次**有放音效的點火**的 `[ts-5s, ts+20s]` 窗口內，`[Plan12_Stats]` 的 `gap_max_ms` 取最大值。如果超過 60ms 的比例，高於**沒放音效的點火**的同樣比例，或者出現 `music_underrun > 0` → 關掉（把 early return 加回去）。其他指標都不用看。
2. **接話率（方向參考）**：放了音效的點火，10 秒內接話率 ≥ holdout 點火（同樣熱、同樣有口白，只差在沒放音效）。這個比較把「口白」這個混淆因素排除了。
3. **負面反應沒增加**：放了音效的點火，60 秒內負面反應的比例 ≤ holdout 點火。
4. **質性**：兩位核心使用者有沒有對音效說過話（正面或負面都算），從 STT log 撈。

後續決定：
- 第 3 條沒過 → 調高 `HEAT_UTT_PER_MIN` 或降低 `SFX_HOURLY_CAP`，再跑一週，不加素材。
- 第 2、3 條都過，**而且放了音效的點火 ≥ 20 次** → 開一次 `/plan-eng-review` 評估要不要做 Approach B。不會因為這些指標就自動升級。

## Reviewer Concerns

Disposition: CONCERNS_RECORDED

Stop: CONVERGENCE

### R2-1 — consistency

**Problem**

> The Playback section is titled '不在點火當下 fork ffmpeg', but it then decodes riser.wav and shoutout.wav '在第一次需要時' (the first time they are needed). The first time they are needed is a fire, inside the song-change window, so the first fire of each asset after every restart still forks ffmpeg in that window. Next Steps 3 also judges the kill gate by looking at the very first SFX fire after restart, which is exactly the fire that still pays the ffmpeg cost. That fire is unrepresentative, and it is the riskiest case.

**Remedy**

> Decode and cache both assets eagerly at cog load or on_ready, in a background task outside any fire window. If the cache is not ready when a fire happens, skip the SFX (logged reason, for example 'not_ready') rather than decoding inline.

### R2-2 — completeness

**Problem**

> Step 1 writes one record per fire in _run_tail_dj, before the dj_meta early return, with sfx=null and reason=baseline. Step 2 puts pick_tail_sfx inside _play_dj_tail_sfx, which only runs after meta is resolved and narration has played. The design never says how the Step 2 record gets its sfx and reason (cold, cooldown, holdout, intimate) when the log line is written before the decision is made. It also does not say whether a fire can produce two records. Rule 2 (no_narration) cannot be reached from _play_dj_tail_sfx, so the 'for complete records' justification only works if pick_tail_sfx is called from _run_tail_dj.

**Remedy**

> Fix the call site and the log point for Step 2. One option: call pick_tail_sfx in _run_tail_dj right after _resolve_tail_dj_meta, write exactly one record with its result, then pass the chosen name to the play step. Another option: move the log write to after the decision and handle the no-meta branch explicitly. Also state the one-record-per-fire invariant.

### R2-3 — clarity

**Problem**

> has_narration is defined only as 'got meta'. But _maybe_play_dj_interjection can still play nothing after meta is resolved: it returns early on intimate mode, on vc None, or on empty text, and play_dj_on_tts_layer can fail. Success Criteria 2 and 3 compare SFX fires with holdout fires 'with the same narration', so a has_narration value that does not mean 'narration was actually queued' weakens that comparison.

**Remedy**

> Define has_narration as whether narration was actually pushed to the TTS layer. For example, have _maybe_play_dj_interjection return a bool and log that value. Alternatively, state explicitly that it means meta-resolved and exclude intimate-mode fires from the comparisons.

### R2-4 — clarity

**Problem**

> The follow-up window (ts, ts+10s] and the negative window (ts, ts+60s] start at the fire ts, but the SFX is queued after the narration on the TTS layer, so it plays several seconds after the fire. Much of the 10s follow-up window can pass while the narration is still playing, before the SFX sounds. Utterances made during the narration then count as 'reactions to the SFX', and reactions after the SFX may fall outside the window.

**Remedy**

> Log the time the SFX was pushed to the mixer. For holdout fires, log the time it would have been pushed (for example, the moment the narration push returns). Anchor both windows on that time instead of the fire ts, or justify why the fire ts is good enough.

### R2-5 — clarity

**Problem**

> pick_tail_sfx is called a pure function, but rules 4 and 5 depend on randomness (HOLDOUT_P) and on play-history state (last play per asset, count in the past hour). The design does not say how that state is passed in, where it is stored (it is probably lost on restart), or whether holdout or cooldown outcomes count toward the hourly cap. Without this, the TDD dispatch to sonnet leaves design choices open, which CLAUDE.md forbids.

**Remedy**

> Specify the signature: an injected random draw (or rng), plus play history passed as input, for example a list of (name, ts) for actual plays only. Name the owner of that history (an in-memory list on the cog, reset on restart, accepted) and state that only actual plays count toward cooldown and the cap.

### R2-6 — completeness

**Problem**

> Round-1 R1-13 asked for intimate mode and any other mute or protected-state guards. Only _intimate_mode was added. play_cached_sfx pushes straight to self._mixer.push_tts, so it bypasses every guard in play_tts. The narration runs inside vc._protected_tts_window(), but the SFX would be pushed outside it. The design does not say how the SFX behaves under /marvin_talk exclusive mode, Echo Guard or mute states, or other TTS suppression states. The project memory records that each mute or drop guard must be handled explicitly.

**Remedy**

> List which suppression states must block the SFX (at least /marvin_talk exclusive mode, and any bot-mute or volume-zero state). Add them to pick_tail_sfx inputs or to the IO shell. Say whether the SFX push happens inside the same protected TTS window as the narration.

### R2-7 — consistency

**Problem**

> Premise 4 still has the heading '先驗證舊限制，再加新功能' (verify the old limit first, then add the feature). But the thing it verifies (pre-decoded static SFX does not worsen frame gap) is measured only after Step 2 ships the full selection feature. There is no step that verifies before adding. The premise now states what is verified, but its ordering claim contradicts Next Steps.

**Remedy**

### R2-8 — feasibility

**Problem**

> Success Criteria 1 relies on joining [Plan12_Stats] gap_max_ms and music_underrun from bot_stdout.log against fire timestamps. The design does not confirm two things: that those stdout lines carry absolute timestamps usable for a [ts-5s, ts+20s] join (the project memory notes that print output in bot_stdout.log is the real source, but its timestamp format is not stated), and that music_underrun is a field emitted in that line.

**Remedy**

> State the exact log line format and the timestamp source used by analyze_dj_tail_fires.py, and confirm that music_underrun exists in [Plan12_Stats] (or name where it comes from). Step 1's 'gap 窗口統計' output should prove the join works on baseline data before Step 2.

### R2-9 — clarity

**Problem**

> The hard kill rule fires when the share of SFX fires with gap_max over 60ms is higher than the same share for no-SFX fires. It sets no minimum count and no tolerance. With only a handful of SFX fires, one spike (for example 1/3 vs 2/40) flips the gate. Meanwhile a 25s window of 5s samples catches the known ~50ms song-change residue for every fire. The rule can trigger on noise, or it can go unevaluated.

**Remedy**

> Add a minimum number of SFX fires before the proportion comparison applies, and a margin or absolute count rule, for example 'kill if 2 or more SFX fires exceed 60ms and the rate exceeds baseline by X'. Keep music_underrun > 0 as the immediate kill condition.

### R2-10 — completeness

**Problem**

> is_human_request depends on the invariant that every non-human enqueue path sets a non-None _lane. The spec cites 'cand.lane' for autopilot. If any candidate's lane can be None, or a future enqueue path forgets _lane, autopilot songs that keep a real name via _attribution_with_suki would be classified as human requests and get shoutout. Nothing in the design enforces or tests this invariant.

**Remedy**

> Before dispatching Step 1, confirm that cand.lane is never None, or treat a None lane from autopilot as non-human. Add a test or assertion that covers each enqueue site: autopilot, themed, associative, story_arc, personal, and human request.

### R2-11 — completeness

**Problem**

> The fire definition covers only _run_tail_dj. The manual-skip path _play_tail_dj_after_skip also resolves DJ meta and plays narration, but it never calls _play_dj_tail_sfx and would not be logged. The design does not say whether skip-triggered narration is deliberately excluded from SFX and from the fire log.

**Remedy**

> State explicitly that the seamless-skip DJ path gets no SFX and is not counted as a fire (or include it), so the analysis population and the behavior are unambiguous.
